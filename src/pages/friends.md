- [Amer Sikira](https://www.linkedin.com/in/amer-sikira-460a96154/) builds websites for startups and SaaS founders. He's also the kind of reader every writer hopes for: he once messaged me to complain that he'd lost a whole evening to my blog.
- [Ben Putano](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ben-putano/) wrote [Great Founders Write](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/63829773-great-founders-write), and I got to edit it. He was also one of the first five people I pitched for my podcast, and he said yes.
- [Douwe Wester](https://douwewester.substack.com/) turns go-to-market for B2B SaaS founders into frameworks you can use, starting from your best customers. His Substack is worth subscribing to.
- [Ellen De Geyndt](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ellen-de-geyndt-b3a53b116/) is the CEO of [AlfaSent](https://www.alfasent.ai/), a Ghent University spin-off that uses AI to read the emotions in customer reviews and feedback. She built the first prototypes herself, as a linguist who taught herself to code.
- [Jona Koekelcoren](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jonakoekelcoren/) went from marketing to chief revenue officer at OpenTheBox, a route not many marketers take.
- [Loki Kapoor-Parsons](https://www.linkedin.com/in/loki-kapoor-parsons-787628135/) coaches at Arsenal's women's academy and grew his TikTok past 210,000 followers in two years. We worked together long enough to invent our own system for labelling voice notes.
- [Ludwig Dumont](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ludwigdumont/) is the CEO and founder of Willow, a social media platform for professional-services firms, where I've run marketing for more than two years. He's also behind Will, the AI agent that writes your LinkedIn posts over WhatsApp.
- [Mike Lamb](https://www.linkedin.com/in/themikelamb/) calls himself an action architect.
- [Nat Miletic](https://cliowebsites.com/) has run Clio Websites, a web design agency in Calgary, since 2007. I wrote for his blog across a string of projects, and I still recommend his SEO course for web developers.
- [Rahul Chawra](https://www.decodestrategy.com/) is a brand strategist in Singapore who runs Decode Strategy Labs. He brought me in on several projects, including the Aspire blog, and his thinking on content ended up in [why startup content strategies fail](/blog/why-startup-content-strategies-fail).
- [Stephane Maes](https://www.linkedin.com/in/maesstephane/) is building Hebbian, a memory layer for a whole company that plugs into whichever AI tools it already uses.

The idea for this page comes from [slashfriends.org](https://slashfriends.org/), which keeps a directory of pages like it.
