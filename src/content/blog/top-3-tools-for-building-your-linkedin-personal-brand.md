LinkedIn has become *the* place for entrepreneurs, sales leaders, and managers to showcase expertise and grow their networks. But consistently creating quality content and engaging on LinkedIn can be tough when you’re busy. Luckily, a new wave of AI-powered tools can help you build a personal brand on LinkedIn with less effort. Below, we highlight three top tools – **HeyWill**, **Scripe**, and **Taplio** – that make LinkedIn personal branding easier. Each has its own style and strengths, so you can find the one that fits your needs.

## 1. [HeyWill.ai](http://heywill.ai) – *Your WhatsApp LinkedIn Ghostwriter*

Working with Will is like having a personal LinkedIn ghostwriter you can text anytime. This tool works entirely through WhatsApp, so creating a post feels as easy as chatting with a friend. Just send Will a voice note or a few bullet points about your idea, and he’ll turn it into a polished LinkedIn post in **under 5 minutes**. Will even learns your writing style by analyzing your past posts, ensuring the content *“sounds”* like you. The result is authentic, on-brand content without the cringe or fluff – and you didn’t have to open any extra apps or dashboards.

**Ideal for:** Busy founders, execs, and sales leaders who have lots of insights to share but little time to polish LinkedIn posts. If you find yourself *too* busy to sit down and write, HeyWill is a lifesaver.

**Standout Features:**

- **WhatsApp Integration** – Operate entirely via chat. No new software to learn; simply drop Will a message on WhatsApp and get a ready-to-post LinkedIn update.
- **AI Copywriter 24/7** – Will is available any time and responds quickly with content. It’s *“like chatting with a personal copywriter”* who never sleeps.
- **Personalized Posts** – By connecting your LinkedIn, Will analyzes up to 50 of your recent posts to learn your tone and voice. Posts it generates feel authentic to you.
- **Quick Publishing** – After drafting, Will can **refine** and even **auto-post** the content to your LinkedIn (with your permission) right from WhatsApp. You can go from idea to live post without leaving your chat.

**Pricing:** HeyWill offers a 7-day free trial to test the waters. After that, it’s around €25/month (early adopters currently pay €15/month) – a straightforward price considering you’re essentially getting an on-demand copywriter. There are no complex tiers; just one affordable plan to keep your LinkedIn presence consistent.

## 2. [Scripe.io](http://scripe.io) – *The All-in-One LinkedIn Content Workspace*

Scripe is a powerful AI-driven **personal branding workspace** for LinkedIn. Think of it as your content creation studio, strategist, and coach combined. Scripe’s platform helps you craft LinkedIn posts that “the algorithm loves” in minutes. It does this by analyzing thousands of viral LinkedIn posts to know what works, then layering **your** expertise on top of that. Unlike generic writing tools, Scripe lets you **train the AI with your own knowledge** (you can upload documents or links as a knowledge base) so the content is highly personalized.

One of Scripe’s coolest features is voice input – you can literally **record a voice memo** of your thoughts, and Scripe will transcribe and transform it into a well-structured LinkedIn post. It’s perfect for those moments when you have a great idea during your commute or between meetings. Scripe also helps with strategy: it provides *personalized prompt questions* each week based on the topics you want to be known for. This keeps your content on-topic and saves you from the “what should I post?” block. Additionally, Scripe includes a content calendar that organizes your posts and ensures you cover all your key topics evenly over time – great for maintaining a balanced personal brand (e.g., mix of educational, personal, industry posts).

**Ideal for:** Founders, consultants, and marketing/sales professionals who want a **strategic approach** to LinkedIn. If you have a team (e.g. a marketing assistant or multiple team members building personal brands), Scripe shines – it supports team collaboration, shared calendars, and even role permissions for larger branding efforts. It’s built “for founders and their teams,” so it scales from solo users to company-wide thought leadership programs.

**Standout Features:**

- **Voice-to-Post Magic** – Talk out your ideas and let Scripe do the writing. Drop in a voice note and Scripe’s AI converts it into a ready LinkedIn post, capturing your spoken enthusiasm in text. No typing needed!
- **Content Inspiration Engine** – Instead of just showing generic trending posts, Scripe asks you tailored questions based on your focus areas to spark ideas. This keeps you creating content that builds your authority, not just chasing viral trends.
- **Intelligent Content Calendar** – Scripe plans and **schedules posts** for optimal times, and its calendar view helps ensure you’re consistently hitting all your core topics. It’s like having a content strategist organizing your posting schedule.
- **Analytics that Learn** – Scripe tracks how your audience engages with each post and actually *learns* from it. Over time, the AI adapts to what works best for *your* style and audience, and even gives you tips to improve future posts. It’s an ongoing coach that makes your content better and better.
- **Team Collaboration** – If you’re working with others, Scripe allows real-time co-editing of posts, feedback within the app, and shared dashboards for multiple LinkedIn profiles. Everyone stays on the same page (literally) when building the company’s brand.

**Pricing:** Scripe has a tiered pricing model. For individual professionals just starting, plans begin around €19.99/month (billed annually). This Starter plan includes core features (limited premium AI posts). The Professional plan (~€39.99/month annual) unlocks unlimited premium AI posts and advanced features – ideal if you’re serious about LinkedIn growth. Teams and businesses can opt for higher plans that support multiple profiles and added services like a dedicated strategist. All plans come with a free trial, so you can try Scripe’s toolkit and see the results before committing.

## 3. [Taplio.com](http://taplio.com) – *The LinkedIn Growth Powerhouse*

Taplio is an **all-in-one platform** to grow your personal brand on LinkedIn, packed with features to create content, build engagement, and even generate leads. It’s widely used by top content creators and businesses to manage their LinkedIn presence. Taplio’s approach combines powerful AI content generation with data-driven insights. For instance, you can enter a topic or keyword and Taplio will show you *thousands of top-performing posts* in that niche, so you never run out of proven content ideas. This viral post library is a goldmine for inspiration – you can filter by industry, popularity, recency, and more.

When you’re ready to write, Taplio’s AI is there to help craft posts. It’s trained on 500+ million LinkedIn posts, which means it has a deep understanding of what works on the platform. You can generate a post (or an outline) with the AI and then tweak the tone or add a call-to-action as needed. The platform also has a slick editor where you can format posts (and even create visually engaging **carousel posts** in a few clicks). Once your content is ready, scheduling is simple – one click to queue it up at the best time for engagement. Taplio will even suggest optimal posting times based on when your audience is most active.

**Ideal for:** Entrepreneurs, sales/marketing managers, and content creators who want **everything in one place** – from content ideation to posting to analytics. If you’re the kind of person who loves digging into data and maximizing results, Taplio provides the tools to not only create content but also *amplify* it. It’s great for those focused on **lead generation and networking** through LinkedIn as well, since it includes features like a relationship builder (to help you find and engage with the right people) and even a lead database on higher plans. In short, if LinkedIn is a key part of your growth strategy, Taplio gives you a comprehensive command center.

**Standout Features:**

- **Huge Content Idea Library** – Taplio curates over 5 million viral LinkedIn posts across industrie. Just input your niche or keywords, and you’ll see what topics and posts are performing best. This makes brainstorming new posts *so* much easier, because you’re learning from content that already proved successful.
- **AI Post Writer & Editor** – Taplio’s AI can **outline and write 100% of your post** if you want, based on your prompts. You can choose a suggested hook, have the AI draft the content, then edit it in-app. The AI is fine-tuned for LinkedIn content, aiming to make your posts engaging and on-trend.
- **One-Click Scheduling & Queue** – Consistency is key on LinkedIn, and Taplio helps by letting you schedule posts with one click. Plan out days or weeks of content, and use the **Kanban view** to organize drafts, scheduled, and published posts visually (so you always know what’s next in the pipeline).
- **Advanced Analytics** – Taplio goes beyond LinkedIn’s basic stats. The dashboard shows which posts are performing best (by views, likes, comments) and tracks your follower growth and profile views over time. These insights help you understand what content resonates with your audience so you can do more of it.
- **Engagement & Networking Tools** – A standout feature is Taplio’s focus on engagement. It has an **AI-powered engagement** tool that helps you find and interact with influential posts in your niche (to grow your visibility), and a Chrome extension that surfaces key metrics and top posts while you browse LinkedIn. Essentially, Taplio not only helps you post content but also ensures you’re actively building relationships on the platform.

**Pricing:** Taplio offers a 7-day free trial on all plans. The pricing is tiered based on features. The **Starter** plan (around $39/month) includes basic post creation, scheduling, and analytics. The **Standard** plan ($65/month) unlocks the full AI content generation capabilities, carousel maker, and the relationship-building tools. For power users or teams, the **Pro** plan ($199/month) provides a higher quota of AI generations and access to a large lead database for outreach. While it’s a bigger investment than the other tools, Taplio is a premium solution aimed at those who treat LinkedIn as a serious growth channel. Many users find the robust feature set worth it for accelerating their LinkedIn results.

## Choosing the Right Tool: Which One Should You Use?

All three of these tools can dramatically boost your LinkedIn personal branding, but the *best* choice depends on your style and goals:

- **[HeyWill](http://www.heywill.ai)** is perfect if you crave simplicity and speed. It’s the *hands-free* option – ideal for the ultra-busy professional who wants to maintain a LinkedIn presence with minimal effort. If you’d love to just voice-message your idea and get a great post ready to go, HeyWill is your buddy. You won’t get advanced analytics or content calendars here, but you **will** get consistent, authentic posts with zero hassle. For a lot of entrepreneurs and sales reps, that’s exactly what they need.
- **[Scripe](http://www.scripe.io)** is the go-to if you’re looking for a **strategic content partner**. It’s more than a writer – it’s a planner and coach. If you’re building a thought leadership presence and want to map out your content pillars, post regularly with a purpose, and maybe involve your team in the process, Scripe has you covered. It strikes a balance between automation and strategy. You’ll spend a bit more time upfront (recording ideas, reviewing suggestions), but the payoff is a well-rounded LinkedIn presence that grows your authority in your domain. For founders and managers serious about *content strategy* (not just individual posts), Scripe is a fantastic choice.
- **[Taplio](http://www.taplio.com)** is the **powerhouse** for those who want *every* feature in one platform. If you’re a data-driven marketer or a sales leader who sees LinkedIn as a key funnel for leads, Taplio will feel like a candy store. It not only helps you write and schedule posts; it also helps you find trending topics, engage with others, and analyze performance in depth. Taplio is best if you plan to invest time into leveraging all these tools – it can do a lot, but you’ll get the most value if you use its content ideas, networking features, and analytics regularly. For those aiming to become LinkedIn influencers or generate significant business through LinkedIn, Taplio can give you an edge (and save you from juggling multiple apps to do the same tasks).

**Conclusion:** In the end, the choice might even come down to personal workflow. Do you want to fire off ideas over WhatsApp and let the AI do the rest? Go with **HeyWill**. Want a guided platform that helps shape your content and learns along with you? **Scripe** is your friend. Need a full-scale LinkedIn command center to turbocharge your content and networking? Try **Taplio**. The good news is that each tool offers a free trial, so you can experiment and see which fits *your* work style best. Whichever you choose, you’ll be leveling up your LinkedIn game and building your personal brand without the overwhelm. Here’s to working smarter and watching those LinkedIn notifications roll in!
